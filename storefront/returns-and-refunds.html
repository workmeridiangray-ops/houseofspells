Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short: if your item arrives damaged, defective or not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos or an unboxing video, and we will replace it or refund you. For unused, sealed items you simply changed your mind about, you can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

1. When you can return or exchange an item

  • Damaged in transit — broken, cracked, chipped, leaking or crushed on arrival.
  • Defective — a manufacturing fault, for example a candle that is badly cracked or has a broken wick, or jewellery with a faulty clasp.
  • Wrong item — you received a different product, size, variant or quantity from the one you ordered.
  • Missing item — an item on your invoice was not in the parcel.
  • Change of mind — the item is unused, unopened, sealed, in its original packaging and returned within 7 days of delivery. Return shipping for change-of-mind returns is paid by the customer.

2. What cannot be returned

For hygiene, safety and the nature of the goods, we cannot accept returns of:

  • Candles that have been lit or used, and opened or used incense, oils, herbs, resins, bundles and other consumable ritual supplies.
  • Jewellery that has been worn, resized, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Items damaged through misuse, drops, heat, water or improper handling after delivery.
  • Products purchased in a sale or marked final sale (unless damaged, defective or wrong).
  • Natural variation in colour, shape, size, banding, veining, inclusions or minor surface marks in crystals and gemstones. These are natural, as noted on the product pages.
  • Tarot, oracle or other readings, spiritual outcomes or personal experiences. Our products are sold as ritual, decorative and meditation items. We make no medical, scientific or guaranteed spiritual claims.
  • Items returned without prior approval or without the original packaging, tags and accessories.

3. How to request a return

  1. Email support@houseofspells.in or message +91 00000 00000 with your order number, the item, the reason, and clear photos. For damage or missing items in transit, please also include the unboxing video showing the sealed parcel being opened.
  2. We review your request within 2 business days and reply with approval or a request for more information.
  3. If approved, we arrange a reverse pickup (for damaged, defective or wrong items), or give you the address to send the parcel to (for change-of-mind returns). Please do not send any parcel before approval.
  4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging with everything you received.
  5. After the item reaches us and passes a quality check (usually 2–3 business days), we process your replacement or refund.

5. Replacements and exchanges

If you would rather have a replacement than a refund, tell us in your request. Replacements are subject to stock. If the item is not available we will refund you in full. Exchanges for a different product are treated as a return plus a new order.

6. Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order at any time before it is dispatched by contacting us as soon as possible. Once the parcel has been handed to the courier, it cannot be cancelled, but you may refuse delivery (this is treated as a return to origin, see the Shipping Policy) or use the return process above once you receive it.

7. Order issues and disputes

If you are unhappy with how we resolved your request, write to our Grievance Officer (details on the Contact page). We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within one month, in line with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
