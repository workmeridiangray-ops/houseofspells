This Privacy Policy explains what personal information House of Spells ("we", "us", "our") collects when you browse or shop on this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent applicable.

By using the website or placing an order, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order and account details — name, email address, mobile number, delivery address, PIN code, and order notes.
  • Messages — what you send us by email, phone, WhatsApp or the contact form.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring page, collected through server logs and cookies.
  • Your cart contents, which are stored in your own browser (local storage) so your cart is not lost.

Payment information

We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials. Online payments are handled by Razorpay Software Private Limited, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway, on their secure systems. We receive only a confirmation of payment status and a transaction reference. For Cash on Delivery orders, no payment details are collected.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, ship and deliver your orders and to send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • To take payment, prevent fraud and handle refunds, returns and complaints.
  • To answer your questions and give customer support.
  • To improve our website, catalogue and service.
  • To send offers and news — only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To comply with legal, tax (GST) and accounting obligations, and to enforce our Terms.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and browser storage to keep your cart and checkout session working, and to understand site usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site (such as the cart) may stop working properly.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep order and invoice records for as long as needed to meet tax and accounting laws (generally up to 8 years). Other data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or anonymised. Marketing contact details are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards — encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access controls, and a managed hosting environment — to protect your data. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you should protect your own devices and passwords.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase your data (we may keep what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices);
  • withdraw consent for marketing or any other consent-based use, at any time;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity; and
  • raise a grievance about how your data is handled.

To exercise any of these, email support@houseofspells.in. We may need to verify your identity first and will respond within a reasonable time and, in any case, within the period required by law.

8. Children

Our website is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites (for example, courier tracking pages or social media). We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept them.
