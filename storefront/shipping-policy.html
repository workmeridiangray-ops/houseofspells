Where we ship

House of Spells currently delivers to addresses within India only, covering all serviceable PIN codes. International shipping is not available yet. We will announce it on this page and on our home page when it launches.

Order processing time

Once your order is placed and confirmed (paid, or accepted for Cash on Delivery), we pick, check and pack it. Orders are normally handed to the courier within 2–5 business days. Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed after midnight, on Sundays or on public holidays begin processing on the next business day.

Some items are made, sourced or restocked in small batches. If your order will take longer than 5 business days to dispatch, we will tell you by phone, WhatsApp or email and you may choose to wait or cancel for a full refund.

Shipping charges

  • Shipping is calculated at checkout from your PIN code and the weight of your parcel, using live courier rates where available.
  • Where live rates are unavailable, a flat charge of ₹79 applies.
  • Free shipping applies on orders with a subtotal of ₹999 or more.
  • Cash on Delivery orders carry no extra COD fee at present. If this changes, the fee will be shown clearly at checkout before you confirm.

The shipping charge you see at checkout is the final shipping charge. There are no hidden fees at delivery. Prices shown on the site are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

Courier partners and tracking

We ship through established courier partners, booked via Shiprocket. When your parcel is dispatched you will receive the courier name and an AWB / tracking number by email, SMS or WhatsApp. You can use it on the courier's website to follow your parcel. If you have not received tracking details 7 business days after ordering, please contact us.

Delivery attempts and Cash on Delivery

  • The courier usually makes up to 3 delivery attempts. Please keep your phone reachable and your address complete.
  • For Cash on Delivery, please keep the exact payable amount ready. If a COD parcel is refused or goes undelivered after all attempts, it returns to us and we may ask for prepayment on your future orders.
  • If a prepaid parcel is returned to us because of a wrong address, an unreachable customer or a refusal, we will refund the product amount, less the outward and return shipping charges we incurred, or re-ship once you pay the shipping again.

Wrong or incomplete addresses

You are responsible for giving a correct, complete address with a valid 6-digit PIN code and a working mobile number. If you need to correct an address, contact us as soon as possible. Changes are only possible before the parcel is handed to the courier.

Damaged, tampered or missing parcels

Crystals, candles and glass items are fragile, and we pack them with protective material. On delivery, please check that the outer packaging is intact. If it looks opened, torn or crushed, please record an unboxing video while opening the parcel and report the problem to us within 48 hours. See our Returns & Refunds policy for what happens next. If a parcel is marked delivered but you did not receive it, tell us within 7 days of the delivery date and we will raise a claim with the courier.

Natural products

Crystals, gemstones, wood and botanicals are natural materials. Size, shape, colour, banding and inclusions vary from piece to piece, so the item you receive may differ slightly from the photograph. This is a natural characteristic, not a defect.

Customs, duties and international orders

Not applicable at this time, as we deliver within India only.
